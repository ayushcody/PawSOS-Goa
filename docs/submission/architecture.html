Conversation and case engine app/conversation.py
1Intake

Photo + location (+ words) open a case: PAW-0041. Outside Goa is refused.

2Triage

Claude Haiku vision returns fixed JSON: species, injury, urgency, aggression, 2 to 3 safe steps. Validated; one retry; on failure a person reviews and the case goes on at high urgency. Never a diagnosis.

3Dedup

PostGIS: an open case within 200 m and 2 hours with a similar animal absorbs the report. Every reporter is told.

4Route

Nearest zone's team gets Accept / Decline. Closed, declined or 10 minutes of silence: next team, then the taluka officer.

5Close the loop

Accepted, on site, resolved: sent to every reporter, with a rescue photo and a rating at the end.

6Bite flow and booking

Wash-the-wound steps and the nearest listed health centre. BOOK picks a camp slot; reminder the day before.

State machine REPORTED → TRIAGED → NEW → ROUTED ↔ ESCALATED → ACCEPTED → ON_SITE → RESOLVED (or MERGED / CANCELLED). The only place a case changes state; every change writes an audit event.
Stores and outputs
Postgres + PostGIS

Cases, reports, photos, teams, zones, alerts, bites, camps, bookings, audit log. Migrations only.

Scheduler

Escalation deadlines stored in the database, so a restart loses nothing. Stuck-case recovery, reminders, demo cleanup.

Officials' dashboard

Leaflet map, case list with filters, case detail and audit trail, sterilisation counts, next-camp suggestion. Phone numbers never shown.

Google Calendar

One calendar per camp for booked slots.
