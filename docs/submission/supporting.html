PawSOS Goa: evidence and status

Supporting document for the Goa AI Hackathon 2026, Track 3. Written 24 November 2026 from the project repository (docs/SPEC.md, docs/PLAN.md, docs/AUDIT.md, docs/DECISIONS.md, docs/site/CLAIMS.md). Every claim below says how it was checked.

1. The problem, in published figures only

  • Goa recorded 19,368 dog bite cases in 2025 (stray and pet), about one every 27 minutes. Source: Health Intelligence Bureau data tabled in the Goa Legislative Assembly (LAQ 097), reported by Goemkarponn, January 2026.
  • In 2025 Canacona, Sankhalim, Valpoi and Mapusa held zero sterilisation camps, while Panaji's partner sterilised 2,918 dogs, Ponda 1,121 and Curchorem-Cacora 243.
  • Each municipality has its own helpline, run by the council or a partner NGO; Panaji's runs 8 am to 8 pm.
  • The channel is proven: Andhra Pradesh's Mana Mitra WhatsApp platform runs 1,126 services and has served 58.2 lakh people.

2. Live run on 24 November 2026

Real Claude triage on a real animal photo (a street dog lying on a pavement at night), model claude-haiku-5-5, through the same code path as WhatsApp:

  • First run (case PAW-0050): answered in about 6 seconds. Urgency "needs attention soon"; three safe steps (keep a distance, keep people away, do not approach or touch); the "not a diagnosis" line.
  • Second run, same photo (case PAW-0051): urgency "does not look urgent"; three safe steps. Urgency from one photo varies between runs; it is guidance, and the routing never depends on it.
  • A second citizen reporting within 200 m was merged into PAW-0051 ("That makes 2 reports").
  • Escalation: Panaji's placeholder team did not answer, then Mormugao's did not; the Tiswadi taluka officer was alerted, accepted, arrived and resolved. Both reporters were told at each step.
  • Public-demo isolation: a second browser session could not see or act on the first session's case ("That case is not in your demo").

5. Not real (placeholders)

  • Partner teams and officers are invented, with invented numbers.
  • Service areas are approximate circles around town centres, not official boundaries.
  • The health centre list is unconfirmed.
  • No measured accuracy, response-time improvement or cost saving is claimed.

6. Safety and privacy design

  • Triage returns a fixed schema, validated with one retry; on failure a person reviews and the case continues at high urgency. The prompt and every message forbid a diagnosis.
  • Every state change goes through one state machine with a transitions table and writes an audit event.
  • Phone numbers are masked in logs and never shown on the dashboard. Reporter words are stripped of links, numbers and hidden characters before reaching a team.
  • The WhatsApp webhook is verified with HMAC; staff pages are behind a password.
  • The public demo gives each visitor private invented numbers in a range no real Indian mobile uses, caps real model calls per visitor and per day, and deletes demo cases after 24 hours.

7. Stack

Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and Alembic, Postgres with PostGIS, APScheduler, Anthropic Claude Haiku (vision, structured output), Leaflet. Source: github.com/ayushcody/PawSOS-Goa. Live demo: goa.ayushchougula.in.
